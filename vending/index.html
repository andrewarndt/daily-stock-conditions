Vending Machine Services

Serving the Columbus and Cincinnati, Ohio area. If you manage a facility and are thinking about adding a vending machine, this page explains what is available and how it works. I place, stock, and service the machines, so there is nothing for you to run or maintain.

Machine types

Drink

Cold beverage machines with water, soda, juice, sports drinks, and energy drinks.

Food

Snack and food machines with chips, candy, granola bars, and other grab-and-go items.

Vapor / Tobacco-Free

Machines for vapor and tobacco-free products, for locations where that fits the clientele.

Product selection can be adjusted to suit the people who use your facility.

How it works

1. Reach out

Tell me about your facility and which kind of machine you are interested in.

2. Site visit

We look at the space, foot traffic, and where a machine would fit best.

3. Placement

I install the machine and stock it with products suited to your location.

4. Ongoing service

Regular restocking and maintenance, with a contact if anything needs attention.

Who it is for

Offices, apartment communities, gyms, shops, warehouses, schools, and event venues in the Columbus and Cincinnati areas. If you have a break room, lobby, or common area with steady traffic, it is worth a conversation.

Interested in a machine at your facility?

Send a short note with your facility type, location, and the machine type you are considering. There is no obligation, and I am happy to answer questions.
