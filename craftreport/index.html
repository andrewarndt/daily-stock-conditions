The goal

Bring pricing power back to the vendor community.

I'm a vendor too. At the first event I ever did, about 10 people came through all day. I made more money selling to the other vendors than to the people who showed up.

The baker who was there had it worse. She had to give away all of her inventory and took a much bigger loss than I did. We paid for that booth based on the event's marketing and its past history. If we had known it was really a $10 event, we might never have gone.

This page is the start of something better: a place where vendors can have confidence in what they're paying for. When we pool real numbers, venues have to price honestly.

A fellow Ohio vendorFounder of CraftReport

Privacy

Your numbers stay yours.

  • Minimum of 3No result is ever shown for an event and category with fewer than 3 reports, so nobody can work out who you are.
  • Totals onlyResults show medians and ranges, never a single vendor's numbers.
  • Email kept apartIf you leave an email, it's stored separately from your sales answers and used only to send the report. It is never sold or shared.
  • Organizers see no raw dataIf an organizer shares this form, they see the same public totals as everyone else.
  • Delete on requestEmail 4aholdingscompany@gmail.com with the event name and date, and your report will be removed.
