How to read this

The numbers, in plain terms.

  • Booth fee: the organizer's published price for one booth for the whole event. "Not published" means the organizer only shares it with applicants, not that it's free.
  • 7× target: a common rule of thumb says a show should bring in about 7 times its booth fee. It's what you'd want to sell for the fee to be worth it.
  • Median sales: the middle vendor report. Half sold more, half sold less. The typical range is the middle half of reports.
  • Sales ÷ fee: how many times the booth fee vendors made back. Green is 7× or more, red is under 3×.
  • Sales for an event and category are only shown once 3 or more vendors have reported, so no one's numbers can be picked out.
