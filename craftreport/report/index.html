The Ohio craft shows to watch in December and January

We checked 47 Ohio craft shows, holiday markets and makers markets for December and January, and found 28 with a confirmed 2026 date. We ranked them on how well they are advertised, how many people are responding to their Facebook events, their track record, how close they are to a major Ohio city, and how clear their vendor information is. How we ranked

Every ranked event had a current organizer, venue, tourism or Facebook event page showing a date in December 2026 or January 2027. A listing site alone wasn't enough. Each event is scored out of 100:

  • advertising reach (25)
  • interaction on its Facebook event (30)
  • history and track record (20)
  • distance from Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Toledo, Akron, Canton or Dayton (15, straight-line miles)
  • how clear the vendor information is (10)

"Facebook interested" is the count Facebook showed on November 19. It measures buzz, not attendance. History marked as the organizer's count comes from the organizer's own "Nth annual" wording. An independent check re-read the official pages for the top 12 and found no wrong dates or cities.

Before you pay

Six questions to ask any organizer

A good organizer will answer these happily. If they won't, that tells you something too.

  1. How many people came last year, and how did you count them?A ticket count or a door clicker beats "about 5,000".
  2. How many vendors will there be, and how many sell what I sell?Ten jewelry booths split the same shoppers ten ways.
  3. How are you promoting it, and how much are you spending?Ask for specifics: paid ads, signs, radio, local news. "We post on Facebook" isn't a plan.
  4. Can I talk to a vendor who did it last year?Better yet, look it up on CraftReport once enough vendors have reported it.
  5. What happens with bad weather or a cancellation?Get the refund or rain-date policy in writing before you pay.
  6. Is the fee for the whole event, or per day?A $70 booth for three days is $210. Compare whole-event costs.

How this report was made

  • Ohio show details (booth fees, dates, juried or not, vendor counts, attendance) come from the organizers' own websites and event listings, gathered by hand. Each show's source link is in the event directory.
  • "Typical" means the median: half the shows charge more, half charge less. Groups with fewer than 3 shows are left out of the charts.
  • A show that lists a per-day fee is counted at its whole-event cost where the number of days is known.
  • Vendor sales results from the CraftReport form are only ever shown as medians and ranges, and only once at least 3 vendors have reported the same show and category.
