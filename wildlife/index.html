About the photographer

I'm Andrew Arndt. I don't plan trips around a shot list; I travel, and I keep a camera close for whatever wanders past. These galleries are what came back with me from the Everglades, the Smokies and Sri Lanka's national parks.

Spending time in these places is a big part of why I care about them, and it's why 10% of the profit from every print is donated directly to the park where it was taken. The parks know best what they need, and giving directly means none of it is lost to a middle organization's overhead. I'd like people to be able to see these places for a long time to come.
