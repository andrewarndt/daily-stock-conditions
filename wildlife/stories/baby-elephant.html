Baby Elephant

Small Wonder

Minneriya National Park, Sri Lanka

In August 2024, my phone rang with exciting news. It was our tracker, calling to say that one of the very rare tuskers had been seen in Minneriya National Park.

Another wildlife photographer and I set off together with one goal: to see that tusker. Out in the wild, we found him, and he wasn't alone. The big tusker was with a massive herd of elephants.

But the most special thing about that herd wasn't the tusker at all. It was the babies. There were so many baby elephants, playing all across the field. The tank was full, the grass was lush and green, and the little ones were having so much fun.

We had travelled all that way for the big tusker. Yet the picture I want to share is of one of the smallest elephants there. Among that whole enormous herd, this was one of the tiniest babies I captured that day.
