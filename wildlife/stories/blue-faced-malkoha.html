Blue-faced Malkoha

Hours for a Heartbeat

Kumana National Park, Sri Lanka

Some animals will sit still and let you take their picture. The Blue-faced Malkoha is not one of them.

This was a very, very busy little bird. He never stayed in one place for more than a second. Hop to one branch, then the next, then another, and by the time the camera found him, he was already gone again.

Most people would have given up and moved on. We didn't. For hours and hours, we followed that same restless bird through the branches, waiting for the one moment he might hold still.

And at last, he did. Just for an instant, the malkoha paused, and the camera clicked.

This is that picture. It may look like a simple photo of a small bird, but behind it are hours of patience, and one very busy malkoha who finally decided to take a rest.
