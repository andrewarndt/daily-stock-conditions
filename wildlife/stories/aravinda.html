Aravinda

A Breath Apart

Yala National Park, Sri Lanka

Aravinda is one of the youngest tuskers I have seen in Yala National Park, but he is already one of the most remarkable.

Look at his tusks. Young as he is, they have grown so long that they reach almost as far as his trunk. And instead of spreading apart, the two tusks grow close together, curving in until their tips almost meet.

They sit so close that Aravinda's trunk has very little room to move between them. It is an unusual way for an elephant to grow, but it gives him a look that is all his own.

Those long tusks, nearly touching at the tips, are what make Aravinda one of the most beautiful tuskers in all of Sri Lanka.
