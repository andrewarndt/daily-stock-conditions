Arjuna

Unlearning

Yala National Park, Sri Lanka

Deep in the jungle of Yala National Park stands an old temple called Sithulpawwa. And for a long time, one of the most famous tuskers in Sri Lanka could almost always be found there. His name is Arjuna.

Arjuna is huge, majestic and beautiful, and people absolutely love him. Visitors to the temple wanted to show him that love, so they fed him: fruit, food, and sometimes even ice cream. They meant well, but it changed him. Arjuna grew so used to those treats that he kept coming back to that one temple in the middle of the jungle, again and again, all the time.

Then COVID came. People stopped travelling, and the temple fell completely empty. There was no one left to feed him.

So Arjuna did something remarkable. He changed his route, went back into the jungle, and started eating wild food again.

This picture was taken after Arjuna had returned to the wild. Here he is, grazing on grass at one of the tanks inside Yala National Park, back to being the wild elephant he was always meant to be.
