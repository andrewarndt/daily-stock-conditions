Babu

Looking for Her

Kumana National Park, Sri Lanka

Babu was a leopard who lived around an estuary inside the national park. At the time, he was one of the major males there, the ruler of a very large territory.

On this trip, I kept running into him. Babu was walking his territory around and around, and over just four days I met him eighteen times. He was searching for his mate.

I had already met her, on the very first day of the trip. She was shy and seemed a little afraid of us. She didn't stay long. The moment she spotted us, she ran off into the jungle.

Babu was the complete opposite. He was fearless. He walked through every corner of his territory without a care for who was watching, determined to find her.

This picture was taken one evening during those four days, as Babu walked across a rock inside the park, still on his search.
