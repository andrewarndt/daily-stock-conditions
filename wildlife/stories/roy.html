Roy

Still There

Kumana National Park, Sri Lanka

This picture is special to me, because it comes from the very first day of my trip, a day when I almost missed everything.

I was still on the road, driving to the national park, when my phone rang around midday. It was my tracker, a ranger who works in the park, and he had news: there was a leopard lying in a tree right next to the park entrance.

I was still a couple of hours away. I told him I wasn't sure I would make it in time. Leopards don't wait for anyone, and by the time I arrived, he would surely be gone. But I wasn't going to give up without trying. I drove as fast as I could.

Two and a half, maybe three hours later, I finally reached the park. And the leopard was still there.

I hadn't had lunch yet, but food could wait. I couldn't risk losing him. I went straight in, headed for the spot, and there he was: Roy, lying in his tree. I took my pictures. Only then did I head back to the entrance and finally eat my lunch.

Then I turned right around and went back in. Roy was still there, and I spent at least another twenty or thirty minutes with him before he finally walked away.

After a long drive, a hungry afternoon, and a race against time, this is the picture I came home with.
