About me

I'm Andrew Arndt, the person behind 4A Holdings Company. I like well-made things: the pattern in a Damascus blade, the weight of a good pocket knife, a diffuser that makes a room feel finished. This store started as a way to put pieces like that in front of people who will use them.

Stock here is small and changes often, so every listing is one I looked over myself. If you have a question about a piece, or you're after something specific, email me and I'll answer personally.
