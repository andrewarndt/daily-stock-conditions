About me

I'm Andrew Arndt, and I started this idea from a simple observation: wedding table decor is beautiful for one evening, and then it is gone. I wanted a centerpiece that people would actually want to take home, and that carries the day's stories along with it.

The engraved vessel is the part I care most about getting right. It should look like it belongs on the table before anyone scans anything. The photos, messages and polls are there to add to the celebration, never to compete with it. I'd love to work with boutiques and planners who feel the same way about the details.

I keep production in small batches on purpose, so I can work closely with each client and venue to deliver the design they want in the material they want. Vessels are my current medium, but I'm not afraid to step outside of them. My specialty is the engraved piece itself, not what it happens to be engraved on.
