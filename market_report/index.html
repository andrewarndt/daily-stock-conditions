About this desk

I'm Andrew Arndt. I follow the markets closely, and I built Market Desk so the things I check every day sit in one place: index levels, sentiment, sector ratios, the Fed and economic data, and bonds.

I care about reading what the data says before forming a view, and about being plain on what's live and what isn't, which is why the page labels its data. This is my own working view of the market, shared for interest. It is not investment advice.
